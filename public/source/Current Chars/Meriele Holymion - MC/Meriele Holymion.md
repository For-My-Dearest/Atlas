## Traits & Features

**Half-Elf Traits:**
- **Fey Ancestry.** Advantage on saving throws against being charmed. Magic cannot put you to sleep.
- **Skill Versatility.** You gain proficiency in two skills (Insight and Perception, for example).

**Druid Traits:**
- **Druidic.** You know Druidic, the secret language of druids. You can leave hidden messages that only other druids understand.
- **Spellcasting.** You cast spells using Wisdom (spell save DC 13, attack bonus +5).
- **Ritual Casting.** You can cast a druid spell as a ritual if it has the ritual tag and you have it prepared. This adds 10 minutes to the casting time but doesn't consume a spell slot.

**Circle of the Moon Features:**
- **Combat Wild Shape.** As a bonus action, you can shapeshift into a beast of CR 1 or lower (no flying speed until level 4). You gain the beast's HP, attacks, and physical stats (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution) but keep your own Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma. You can stay in beast form for 2 hours.
- **Circle Forms.** You can transform into higher CR beasts as you level: CR 2 at level 6, CR 3 at level 9, etc.
- **Wild Shape (2 uses).** Recover both uses on a short or long rest. When you revert from beast form (whether voluntarily or at 0 HP), you return with the same HP you had before transforming.

## Bonus Actions

**Combat Wild Shape**
- _Effect:_ You transform into a beast of CR 1 or lower.
- _Limitations:_ No flying speed until level 4.
- _Duration:_ 2 hours, or until you revert (bonus action) or drop to 0 HP.
- _What you keep:_ Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, skill proficiencies, saving throws.
- _What you gain:_ Beast's Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, HP, natural armor, attacks, and special senses (like darkvision or keen smell).
- _What you lose:_ Ability to cast spells (unless you have a feat that says otherwise), ability to speak, ability to use hands for fine manipulation.
